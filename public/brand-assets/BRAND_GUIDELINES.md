## 1. Brand Essence & Visual Positioning

The visual identity for **Yassa Ashraf Albir** is designed to project a forward-looking, highly technical, and disciplined professional persona across **Cybersecurity**, **Software Development**, **IT Operations**, and **Data Analytics**.

- **Core Emblem**: The **YA Monogram** — a precision-engineered geometric mark merging the letters **Y** and **A**.
- **Geometry**: Parallel $66^\circ$ diagonal trajectories creating dynamic tension between the branches of the Y and the left leg of the A, meeting seamlessly at the baseline.
- **Dual Blue Palette**:
  - **Letter Y**: `#1677FF` (Primary Electric Blue) representing security, infrastructure, and core compute.
  - **Letter A**: `#54A6FF` (Light Sky Blue / Cyan-Blue Accent) representing agility, data insights, and software interfaces.

## 5. Usage & Implementation Rules

1. **Clear Space**: Maintain minimum clear space around the YA symbol equal to the width of the vertical stem ($0.15 \times \text{height}$) on all four sides.
2. **Minimum Sizes**:
   - Primary Horizontal Logo: Minimum width $120\text{px}$ on screen ($30\text{mm}$ in print).
   - Standalone YA Symbol: Minimum size $16\text{px}$ (using the optical favicon variant).
3. **Contrast Integrity**:
   - On dark surfaces (`#080D16` to `#1E293B`), use the primary dual-blue variant (`#1677FF` + `#54A6FF`) with `#F4F7FB` text.
   - On pure white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`), use the deep navy variant (`#080D16`) with electric blue accent (`#1677FF`).
   - For monochrome single-ink documents, use `logo-horizontal-monochrome-black.svg` or `logo-horizontal-monochrome-white.svg`.
